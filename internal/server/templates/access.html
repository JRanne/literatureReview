Manual settings

Find it in any proxied library link: the part before the publisher URL. Finnish examples: https://libproxy.helsinki.fi/login?url=, https://libproxy.aalto.fi/login?url=, https://ezproxy.utu.fi/login?url=, https://ezproxy.jyu.fi/login?url=, https://libproxy.tuni.fi/login?url=, https://ezproxy.uef.fi/login?url=, https://login.ezproxy.ulapland.fi/login?url=. (Verify yours on the library website.)
How to get it: 1) open a proxied link in your browser and log in (Haka/SSO); 2) press F12 → Application (Chrome) or Storage (Firefox) → Cookies → the proxy domain; 3) copy the cookie named ezproxy (sometimes also ezproxyl/ezproxyn; paste them as name=value; name2=value2). You can also paste a cookies.txt export. Sessions usually expire after a few hours of inactivity; when downloads start failing with "redirected to login", paste a fresh cookie. The cookie is stored only in data/settings.json on this machine.

Scimago Journal Rank (quartiles) {{if .ScimagoSet}}{{.ScimagoCount}} journals loaded{{else}}not loaded{{end}}

Scimago blocks automated downloads, so fetch the file yourself: open scimagojr.com/journalrank.php, click Download data (bottom right; a semicolon-separated CSV of all ~30,000 journals), and upload it here. Venue ratings then include the SJR best quartile (Q1 raises a venue to good, Q2 to basic; Q3/Q4 are shown but do not raise the tier). Re-rate venues afterwards.

What else raises the PDF yield

  • Campus network or VPN: the "publisher (direct)" step then succeeds for subscribed content without any proxy.
  • Publisher patterns: for Springer, Elsevier, Wiley, Taylor & Francis, SAGE, ACM, IEEE, Emerald, INFORMS, World Scientific, SSRN, Nature, PLOS and Frontiers the app derives the direct PDF endpoint from the DOI and tries it both directly and through the proxy.
  • Manual upload: on the PDFs step every failed paper has an open via proxy link and an upload field, so nothing is out of reach.
